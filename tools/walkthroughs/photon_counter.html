Tagger Run, Line by Line

How phoq's time tagger turns weak laser pulses on two single-photon detectors into click records, counts and coincidences. The walk follows one run through PhotonCounter.acquire() and the detector methods it calls, detect(), photons(), click_probability(), dead_bins() and blind_after(), then the result's counts and coincidences(). Every mark on the screen comes from the arrays those lines produced on the bench below: the lines were replayed with the same random seed and checked against acquire() itself, click for click.

Keys: ← and → step, Space plays or pauses. Data: phoq's own PhotonCounter and SinglePhotonDetector on a bench built for this page. The run is short (2000 bins) and cut into two chunks of 1000 so that the dead time's carry-over between chunks happens on screen; the seed was picked so that it removes a click.
