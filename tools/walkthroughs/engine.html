Engine Run, Line by Line

How phoq gets from a setup to the current a detector puts out, on the simplest bench with a modulator, examples/modulated_link.py. The walk follows setup.run() through trace(), which finds every route the light takes, and synthesize(), which puts each route's field on a time grid, then through the modulator's drive_voltage() and transmission() to Photodiode.detect(). Every curve on the screen is the array that line produced: the lines were replayed with setup.run()'s own random draws, checked against the functions themselves, and give the CH1 trace setup.run() returns, sample for sample.
