ESA Sweep, Line by Line

How phoq's RF spectrum analyzer turns a detector's voltage into the trace on its screen. The walk follows one sweep through record(), acquire(), _signal() and _detect() in the order they run. Every curve on the screen is the array that line produced on the bench below: the lines were replayed with the same random seed and checked against acquire() itself, to 10⁻¹³ dB.

Keys: ← and → step, Space plays or pauses. Data: phoq's own SpectrumAnalyzer on the laser-noise bench from examples/laser_noise.py (one input, 16 dB), with the laser switched to a 500 kHz sine so a tone sits on the noise. The display uses 201 points instead of the default 1001, so each display point spans several bins and the detector modes differ visibly.
