Scope Sweep, Line by Line

How phoq's oscilloscope turns light on a photodiode into a triggered, digitized trace with measurements. The walk follows one sweep through acquire() and the helpers it calls, record(), _front_end(), _digitize() and measure(), in the order they run. Every curve on the screen is the array that line produced on the bench below: the lines were replayed with the same random seed and checked against acquire() itself, sample for sample.

Keys: ← and → step, Space plays or pauses. Data: phoq's own Oscilloscope on a laser → photodiode bench built for this page. The scale and trigger level were chosen the way the lab's Autoset picks them (peak / 6 rounded up a 1-2-5 step, level at half the peak), with the trigger switched to edge so the trigger search runs. A photodiode's output is a current, so CH1 reads amperes.
