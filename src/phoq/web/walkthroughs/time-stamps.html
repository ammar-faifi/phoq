Time Stamps, Line by Line

How Bob's station in COW with vacuum decoys turns his time tagger's stamps into Alice's slots, the way a bench reads its tagger. The walk follows one block through Bob.measure(), read_tagger(), CounterResult.timestamps(), slot_clicks() and phoq.timing's clicked_slots() and fold(), then events(), which makes one event of each pair. Two steps visit Alice's side, carving() and the AWG's _shape(), to show where a slot's center sits. Every mark on the screen comes from the arrays those lines produced on the bench below: the lines were replayed and checked against read_tagger() and events() themselves, slot for slot.

Data: phoq's COW-vacuum bench from examples/cow_vacuum with the tests' bright settings, μ = 0.5 and no hold-off, so that a 20 µs block holds a couple of hundred clicks. The walk reads block 1 so that the block's offset shows in start. The bench is COW vacuum's own, so its stamps carry the PDM-IR's 200 ps of timing jitter. They are still partly the simulator's: the tagger adds no jitter of its own, and the delay compensation puts the session's first pulse at t₀ = 0, so Bob does not have to find t₀.
